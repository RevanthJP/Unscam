      What gives a scam away

      Scammers use fear and hurry. These are some of the signs we listen for.

      • Asking for your OTP or PIN
      • “You will be arrested”
      • KYC updates by link
      • Screen-sharing apps
      • “Move money to a safe account”
      • Unknown numbers acting as your bank
      • Fees to claim a prize
      • “Don’t tell anyone”
